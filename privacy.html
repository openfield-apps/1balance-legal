Last updated: October 2026

1 Balance is a personal expense-tracking and budgeting app. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it goes, and the choices you have.

In short: your financial entries live on your device first. If you sign in and turn on cloud sync, they are also backed up to your account on Google Firebase. We do not sell your data, show ads, or ask for full card numbers or bank logins.

Publisher: Minna Hassan, contact supportopenfieldstudio@gmail.com.

Information we collect

Account information

When you create an account or sign in with email, Google, or Facebook, we receive your email address, display name, username, and profile photo (from the sign-in provider or one you choose yourself).

Financial tracking data you enter

  • Transactions: amounts, categories, dates, notes.
  • Payment methods: a nickname, provider, balance, and only the last 4 digits of a card.
  • Bills and subscriptions: names, amounts, renewal dates, billing cycles.
  • Budgets and saving goals.
  • Split bills: group names, member names, and allocations. Names of other people you enter are stored as you typed them; please enter only what you have a reason to share.

What we do not collect

1 Balance is not a payment service. It never asks for or stores full card numbers, CVV codes, expiry dates, bank logins, or banking credentials. It does not connect to your bank.

Device information

To deliver push notifications, Firebase Cloud Messaging assigns your device a token. Network requests also reveal your IP address to the services described in Section 4.

How your data is stored

  • On your device: transactions, payment methods, bills, budgets, goals, and settings are stored in the app’s private storage on your phone (Hive and SharedPreferences).
  • Cloud sync (optional): if you are signed in and enable sync, transactions, wallets, bills, and budgets are copied to Google Cloud Firestore, tied to your account ID. Google encrypts Firestore data in transit and at rest.
  • App lock PIN: your PIN is kept on your device and is not sent to our servers.
  • Biometrics: fingerprint or face unlock is handled entirely by your device’s operating system. 1 Balance only receives a success or failure result and never sees, stores, or transmits biometric data.

No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and rights

  • Edit your profile and edit or delete any transaction, bill, card, budget, or goal at any time.
  • Turn cloud sync and notifications off.
  • Delete your account: use Settings → Delete Account in the app or email us at the address above. We will delete your account and the cloud data linked to it, and you can clear local data by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage.

Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to access, correct, export, or erase your data. Email us and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Retention

Data stays on your device until you delete it or remove the app. Cloud data stays until you delete it or your account. Backups held by Google may take a short time to clear after deletion.

Children

1 Balance is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

International transfers

Firebase and Google may process data on servers outside your country. By using cloud sync you accept that transfer.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
